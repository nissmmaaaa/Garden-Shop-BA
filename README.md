# Garden Shop Business Analysis

**Tools:** MySQL, SQL, Microsoft Power BI

## 1. Project Overview

Garden Shop Business Analysis is a data analytics project focused on understanding sales performance, customer behaviour, product performance and inventory management for a retail garden shop.

The project uses relational data stored in MySQL and transforms the analysis into an interactive, three-page Power BI dashboard to help business stakeholders make informed decisions.

## 2. Business Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and order performance.
- Understand customer activity and purchasing behaviour.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Monitor stock availability and inventory value.
- Identify business problems and recommend improvements.

## 3. Dataset

The dataset contains six relational tables:

- Customers
- Orders
- Order Items
- Products
- Categories
- Suppliers

**Dataset size:** 130 records across six tables.

## 4. Tools and Technologies

- **MySQL:** Database storage and SQL-based analysis.
- **SQL:** Data retrieval, filtering, aggregation, joins, subqueries, CTEs and window functions.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Data modelling, DAX measures and interactive dashboard development.

## 5. Dashboard Pages

### Page 1: Sales Overview

Analyses total orders, shipped orders, shipped revenue, quantity sold, order status distribution and revenue by product.

### Page 2: Customer Insights

Explores total customers, active and inactive customers, customer registrations by year and top customers by shipped spending.

### Page 3: Product & Inventory Insights

Monitors total products, low-stock products, out-of-stock products, inventory value, revenue by category, quantity sold by product and current stock levels.

## 6. Key Business Findings

- The shop recorded 24 orders, of which 17 were shipped.
- Shipped revenue amounted to **$885.50**.
- A total of 65 units were included in shipped orders.
- The business has 20 customers, including 16 active and 4 inactive customers.
- Six products were below their reorder levels.
- One product was completely out of stock.
- The total inventory value was approximately **$4.76K**.
- Four customers had never placed an order.

## 7. Business Recommendations

1. **Customer Retention:** Run targeted campaigns to encourage inactive customers to return.
2. **Customer Acquisition:** Encourage customers who have never ordered to make their first purchase.
3. **Inventory Management:** Prioritise restocking products below their reorder levels, especially out-of-stock products.
4. **Product Promotion:** Promote high-performing products to improve sales.
5. **Supplier Evaluation:** Review supplier performance and investigate suppliers with no products currently assigned.

## 8. Project Outcome

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard that presents sales, customer and inventory insights in a clear visual format. The project demonstrates practical SQL querying, relational data analysis, DAX calculations, data modelling and business-oriented reporting.
